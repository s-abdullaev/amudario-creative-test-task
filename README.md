# Creative Content Producer Test Task — Amudar.io

Welcome — and thanks for your interest. We're hiring a **Creative Content Producer** to join [Amudar.io](https://amudar.io) in Tashkent. The full role and offer are in [JOB_DESCRIPTION.md](./JOB_DESCRIPTION.md) (English / Русский / Oʻzbekcha).

This exercise is short, open, and almost entirely about taste.

---

## The brief

Make a **video, no more than 60 seconds long**, in which you:

1. **Introduce yourself.**
2. **Show us a few of your past works** — clips, screenshots, voiceover, whatever fits.
3. **Tell us about the most creative thing you have ever done** — anything in your life, work-related or not.

That's it.

### Rules

- **Length**: ≤ 60 seconds. Strict — we stop watching at 60.
- **Language**: English, Русский, or Oʻzbekcha. Your choice — pick the one you create best in.
- **Format**: vertical or horizontal, your call. Any aspect ratio, any frame rate, any codec people can play.
- **Tools**: anything. CapCut, Premiere, Higgsfield, generative AI, your phone, a borrowed DSLR, paper cutouts — we don't care.
- **Style**: you have full creative freedom. Edit it, score it, animate it, hand-draw over it — whatever makes it yours.

### The one thing we care about

**Blow our mind with your creativity.**

---

## How to submit

- Upload your video to YouTube / Vimeo / Google Drive / Telegram. If you set it private, make sure we can actually play it (unlisted is usually safer).
- Send the link to **info@amudar.io** *or* Telegram **[@s_abdullaev_uk](https://t.me/s_abdullaev_uk)**.
- Subject line: `Creative Content Producer Test Task — [Your Name]`.
- Mention roughly **how many hours** you spent on it. No deadline pressure.

---

## What we evaluate

- Is the video **memorable**?
- Did you make a clear decision about **what kind of creator you are**?
- Do your **past works** back up your storytelling?
- Did you respect the **60-second cap**?
